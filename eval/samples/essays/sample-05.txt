Prompt: Compare renting and buying a home for a young professional.
Renting and buying a home are both options for young professionals.

Renting is flexible. If you get a job in another city you can move when your lease ends. You also do not pay for repairs, the landlord does. However rent money is gone forever and you build no equity.

Buying a home is a big investment. You need a down payment which can take years to save. Monthly mortgage payments can be lower than rent in some cities. House prices always go up so buying is basically free money over time.

Interest rates matter too. When rates are high the monthly payment is much bigger.

Both have pros and cons. It depends on the person.
