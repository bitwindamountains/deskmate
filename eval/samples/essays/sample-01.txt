Prompt: Should high schools ban phones during class? Take a position and support it.
Phones should be banned in high school classrooms because they distract students from learning.

Many students check social media during lessons. When a notification arrives, it is hard to ignore, and students lose track of what the teacher was explaining.

Some people say phones help with research. However, schools already provide computers, so students do not need their own phones in class.

In conclusion, banning phones would help students focus and get better grades.
