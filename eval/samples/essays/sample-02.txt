Prompt: Should universities make attendance mandatory? Argue for or against.
Attendance is something that a lot of people have opinions about and there are many sides to it.

Students who go to class get better grades. Everyone knows this, it is just common sense. When you sit in the lecture you hear what the professor thinks is important and you can ask questions right away.

On the other hand some students work part-time jobs. They might need the money for tuition or to help their families, making attendance mandatory would punish them for something that is not their fault. Also some lectures are just the professor reading slides that are already online.

Online recordings are a good thing. My cousin watched all her lectures at double speed and she graduated with honors. Technology has changed how we learn and universities should accept this.

In conclusion attendance has good and bad sides and every university should decide what is best for them.
