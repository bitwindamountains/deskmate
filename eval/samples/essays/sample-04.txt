Prompt: Personal statement: describe a challenge you faced and what it taught you.
I have always been a hardworking and passionate person who loves to learn new things.

In my second year of high school my father lost his job. I started selling snacks before class to help pay for my brother's school supplies. I woke up at five every morning to prepare the food. Some days I was so tired that I fell asleep in math class, and my grades dropped.

My teacher noticed and asked me to stay after class. Instead of scolding me, she helped me make a schedule. I learned to study on the jeepney ride home and to say no to extra orders when I had exams.

This experience taught me many things. It taught me about responsibility, time management, family, hard work, and never giving up. I believe these qualities will make me a great nursing student.
