## Tasks
1. **Swipe in triage (B2.5).** Swipe the card right to keep, left to discard (the same actions as the arrow keys). `touch-pan-y` leaves vertical scrolling to the page. Commit: `feat: swipe to keep or discard in triage`.
2. **Top 5 after three in a row (spec J5).** When the student's last three answers to a priority (Got it or I disagree) were all "Got it", the panel and Focus mode show 5 priorities instead of 3. Ruling: items the re-check marks addressed don't count, because they have no time order to form a streak from; "Got it" is the student's own action. Commit: `feat: show five priorities after three addressed in a row`.
3. **Growth snapshot on Write (A-S1).** "Your top strength: … Working on: …" from the growth profile, when there is enough data. Commit: `feat: growth snapshot on the Write page`.
4. **Start at hint 2 for a recurring weakness (spec J7).** After 3 essays, a category where the student's hint reliance averages level 4 or more opens its items at hint 2. Commit: `feat: start at hint 2 for a recurring weakness`.
5. **Improve with AI in the deck editor (B-S8).** The card editor gets "Suggest a rewrite", reusing `/api/cards/improve`. Commit: `feat: suggest a rewrite in the deck editor`.
6. **Weekly activity on Today (B-S1).** Reviews per day for the last 7 days in the student's time zone. No streak, by owner decision. Commit: `feat: weekly activity on Today`.
7. **Success metrics (Part F).** `docs/metrics.sql`: saved, read-only queries for the F1/F2 metrics the data supports, including time to first card (first card's `created_at` minus the source's). Commit: `docs: queries for the success metrics`.
8. **Creative writing check (A7).** When the essay type is argumentative, expository, compare-contrast or research and the text reads like a poem (many short lines), the form asks the student to confirm or pick another type before any AI call. Commit: `feat: ask before analysing a poem as an essay`.
9. **Custom rubric (A2.1).** An optional rubric, one criterion per line ("Name: what it looks for"), 2–8 criteria scored 1–4, stored as `essays.rubric`. Analysis, rubric scores, the self-assessment and compare use it. Probe and ledger row. Commit: `feat: score essays against the student's own rubric`.
10. **Card types on import (B-S2).** Choose which of Q&A, cloze and "Explain why" to make (`sources.card_types`). Generation adds one user-message line when not all three are chosen, and drops cards of other types. Probe and ledger row. Commit: `feat: choose card types on import`.
