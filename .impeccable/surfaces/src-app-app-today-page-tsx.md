# Today and the app shell

Scope: the (app) shell (nav, page frame, panels, buttons, fields) and the Today screen. Mode: Operate. Mobile first.
Audience and job: an adult student opens Scholar for a short daily session, sees what today holds, and starts the first thing.
Chosen direction: The Study Desk (owner choice 2026-10-25, PR #48), replacing Mixtape J-Card (decision round 2026-10-25, re-roll 1), which felt misleading. Memorable moment: today's study as one index card, with the day's minutes marked in highlighter.
Unresolved: hand-lettered display face is for headings only; whether beta students find it legible enough.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Today's study is one index card on your desk: reviews, then writing, with the minutes you set marked in highlighter. Every object is something a student uses to study; no metaphor. Refuses the category's white-cards-with-indigo dashboard and stat tiles.
OWN-WORLD: Ballpoint blue ink on white card stock, faint blue ruling, one printed accent (brand-stripe red) for the current place only, one lime highlighter for marking. Hand-lettered block caps for headings only, Atkinson Hyperlegible for everything read. Square-ish 4-6px corners, 1.5px ink rules, no shadows. A fanned stack of index cards on the desk ground.
STORY: The student sees today's card (how many reviews and how long, how much writing time is left), trusts it fits their time, and presses Start review.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Hand-lettered "Today" on a doubled ink rule; the index card: the date in hand caps with "N min a day" and the stripe, Reviews with card count and running time right-aligned, Start review; Essays with the minutes left; highlighter strokes at the foot showing reviews and writing against the daily minutes. Exams below as a ruled list. Bottom tab bar on white, current tab in red with a red bar.
FORM: The Study Desk (DESIGN.md), owner-chosen over the earlier Mixtape J-Card (seed key 7144af7c, re-roll 1).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
