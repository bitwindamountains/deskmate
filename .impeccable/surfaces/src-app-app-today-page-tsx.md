# Today and the app shell

Scope: the (app) shell (nav, page frame, panels, buttons, fields) and the Today screen. Mode: Operate. Mobile first.
Audience and job: an adult student opens Scholar for a short daily session, sees what today holds, and starts the first thing.
Chosen direction: Mixtape J-Card (decision round 2026-10-25, re-roll 1). Memorable moment: today's study read as a tape with two timed sides.
Unresolved: hand-lettered display face is for headings only; whether beta students find it legible enough.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Today's study is a mixtape you made for yourself: side A review, side B writing, timed to fit the minutes you set. Refuses the category's white-cards-with-indigo dashboard and stat tiles.
OWN-WORLD: Ballpoint blue ink on white inlay stock, faint blue ruling, one printed accent (brand-stripe red) for the current place and the side letters. Hand-lettered block caps for headings only, Atkinson Hyperlegible for everything read. Square-ish 4px corners, 1.5px ink rules, no shadows.
STORY: The student sees the tape for today (what is on each side and how long), trusts it fits their time, and presses Start review.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Hand-lettered "Today" with an ink rule; the tape panel: Side A Review with card count and running time right-aligned, Start review; Side B Write with the open draft; a tape bar at the foot showing planned minutes of the daily budget. Exams below as a ruled list. Bottom tab bar on white with a red stripe, current tab in red.
FORM: Mixtape J-Card, challenger chosen over the assigned Computation Pad; seed key 7144af7c (re-roll 1).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
